Private by design

Need your account details?
Share them in one tap.

Name, account number, IFSC and branch go out together, and never a password. Everything is encrypted on your phone.

Everything it does

Your paperwork, sorted

Every bank in one place

Account number, IFSC, net banking logins and security answers, each under its bank's logo. The branch fills in from the IFSC.

Scan with Google's scanner

Aadhaar, PAN, cheques, passbooks and cards. A card's front and back are kept together as one picture.

Share or download in a tap

Send a document or your account details straight from its card.

Locked with your PIN

Encrypted on the phone with a hardware-backed key. Screenshots are blocked. Fingerprint unlock is optional.

Backed up to your own Drive

An encrypted copy in your Google Drive's hidden app folder after every change. Restore on a new phone with your PIN.
